5 FACTS ABOUT LIONS

They live in groups called prides

Unlike most other cats, lions form family units of 10 to 15 members (and sometimes up to 40) consisting of related females, their cubs, and a few males.

Females do most of the hunting

Lionesses work together in teams to stalk and ambush large prey like zebras and wildebeest, while males mostly protect the pride's territory.

They are very loud

A male lion's roar can be heard up to 5 miles (8 km) away and reaches a volume of 114 decibels due to their flat vocal cords.

They sleep a lot

Lions are the laziest big cats and spend about 16 to 20 hours every day resting or sleeping.

They don't live in jungles

Despite being called the "king of the jungle," wild lions actually live in open savannas, grasslands, and plains.
